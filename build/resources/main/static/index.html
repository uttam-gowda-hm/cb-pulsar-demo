Try it: Start traffic → make vendor slow → watch the left pool saturate and its backlog climb, while the right pool's breaker trips OPEN and negative-acks messages for Pulsar to redeliver later, instead of blocking any thread → then click "vendor recovers" and watch the breaker go HALF_OPEN → CLOSED, and the deferred messages come back around.
